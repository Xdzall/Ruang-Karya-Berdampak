REAL STORIES

The Stories Behind the Numbers

Behind every statistic is a human story of empowerment and transformation.

KLINIK DIGITAL

Protecting Rural Communities from Online Fraud

Through regional language modules in Minang and Javanese, over 2,000 local merchants learned to identify phishing attempts and secure their digital store accounts effectively.

— Bu Maria, Pedagang Pasar Manado
VOKASI BISA

Future Skills for Vocational Graduates

Connecting vocational students with AI and digital media production skills, enabling 85% of program graduates to secure employment or start freelance digital creative agencies within 6 months.

— Ahmad, Alumni SMK Grafika
JEJAK BAIK

Mobilizing Youth in Disaster Resilience

Over 500 active youth volunteers mobilized to build emergency learning centers and provide psychological first aid and clean water systems in disaster-affected areas.

— Rian, Relawan Jejak Baik
