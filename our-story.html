JOURNEY & PHILOSOPHY

It Started With a Question

Not a legal document. A question: How can knowledge genuinely improve people's lives?

OUR ORIGIN (2018)

From a Community Initiative to a National Movement

In 2018, as Indonesia's digital transformation accelerated rapidly, access to information expanded — but so did misinformation, online polarization, and unequal digital competencies.

Recognizing that these challenges required more than technical solutions, a group of educators, communication practitioners, researchers, and community leaders initiated Klinik Digital. Their mission was simple yet ambitious: to strengthen digital literacy while empowering people to become responsible creators and informed citizens.

As the scope of collaboration widened across universities, government, and media, so did the realization: Indonesia didn't need just one more program. It needed an institution capable of connecting knowledge, education, and humanitarian action across multiple sectors.

That vision led to the establishment of Yayasan Ruang Karya Berdampak (RKD Foundation).

NAME PHILOSOPHY

What "Ruang Karya Berdampak" Means

Our name is our philosophy. Every word reflects our purpose.

RUANG

Space & Ecosystem

A space is more than a location. It is an ecosystem where ideas can emerge, people can collaborate, differences become strengths, and innovation becomes possible.

KARYA

Action & Work

Ideas gain value only when translated into action: research, books, education, technology, community programs, public policy, and humanitarian service.

BERDAMPAK

Public Impact

Impact is our ultimate measure of success: whether people become more capable, communities more resilient, and society stronger long after a project ends.

RKD MANIFESTO

Every Great Change Begins With A Space

We believe knowledge is one of humanity's greatest trusts. Knowledge that remains stored serves only a few. Knowledge that is shared empowers many. Knowledge that is translated into action transforms society.

Inspired by the principles of rahmah (compassion), amanah (trust), ihsan (excellence), and maslahah (public benefit), every initiative we build must answer one simple question:

"Will this leave society better than before?"

If the answer is yes, we move forward. If not, we rethink.
