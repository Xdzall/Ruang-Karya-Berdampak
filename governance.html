ACCOUNTABILITY & INTEGRITY

Trust Is Built Before It's Measured

How we work matters as much as what we do. Every decision, partnership, and resource entrusted to us carries a responsibility to create real public value.

ORGANIZATIONAL ARCHITECTURE

How We're Governed

A collaborative institutional model balancing strategic leadership, expert guidance, and professional management.

Board of Trustees

Providing strategic direction and safeguarding the Foundation's mission and long-term stewardship.

Supervisory Board

Ensuring institutional accountability, compliance, ethics, and organizational integrity.

Executive Leadership

Translating strategy into active programs, partnerships, and operations.

Advisory Council

Independent experts and practitioners offering strategic insights across disciplines.

Secretariat

Professional team running finance, program execution, communications, and MEL day-to-day.

OUR COMMITMENTS

Five Core Governance Commitments

Integrity

Non-negotiable in every decision. Honesty and ethical conduct guide all operations regardless of circumstances.

Transparency

Open reporting on programs, partnerships, financial statements, and institutional performance.

Accountability

We report our shortcomings as openly as our wins. Accepting full responsibility for public outcomes.

Independence

Collaborating widely across government, industry, and civil society without compromising mission or integrity.

Sustainability

Every strategic decision is weighed for its long-term social, environmental, financial, and institutional impact.

Digital Governance

Promoting human-centered AI, digital ethics, data privacy protection, and inclusive technology.

ETHICS & PROTECTION

Safeguarding & Ethics

We protect the dignity, privacy, and safety of everyone we work with — especially children, young people, and vulnerable groups. This includes responsible use of AI and personal data, anti-corruption practices, and zero tolerance for exploitation or abuse.

Measuring What Matters (MEL)

Every program is tracked through Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL). We assess not just what was delivered, but who benefited, how behavior changed, and what lessons inform future work.
