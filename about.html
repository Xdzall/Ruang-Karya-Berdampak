About RKD Foundation

An independent Indonesian nonprofit advancing education, strengthening communities, and creating public impact through collaboration.

WHO WE ARE

Bridging Knowledge and Community Action

Yayasan Ruang Karya Berdampak (RKD Foundation) is founded on the belief that knowledge should never remain confined within classrooms or institutions. We transform research, innovation, and lived experience into practical initiatives that improve people's lives.

We don't run isolated projects. We build ecosystems — connecting academia, government, industry, civil society, media, and local communities to turn research and lived experience into solutions that actually reach people.

OUR CHARACTERISTICS

What Makes Us Different

Our approach is built upon five defining characteristics that guide every initiative we develop.

Knowledge-Driven

Every initiative starts with evidence, research, and continuous learning.

Community-Centered

Communities are not beneficiaries — they are active partners.

Collaborative

No single institution has every answer. Better solutions emerge together.

Future-Oriented

We prepare society to respond to technological and social transformation.

Impact-Focused

Success is measured in lives changed, not merely activities completed.

OUR VISION

A World Where Knowledge Creates Lasting Public Impact

A future where every community — regardless of where they start — has real opportunities to learn, collaborate, innovate, and shape the change ahead.

STRATEGIC COMMITMENTS

Our Mission

We turn knowledge into public impact through six strategic commitments.

01

Advance Education

Promoting lifelong learning, digital & vocational literacy, leadership, and knowledge accessibility across generations.

02

Strengthen Communities

Strengthening local capacity, resilience, and cultural wisdom through collaboration and mutual learning.

03

Accelerate Social Innovation

Transforming research into practical solutions through technology, entrepreneurship, and community participation.

04

Promote Humanitarian Values

Protecting vulnerable communities and strengthening social solidarity inspired by compassion and dignity.

05

Cross-Sector Collaboration

Connecting government, academia, industry, civil society, media, and local communities as a trusted connector.

06

Sustainable Public Impact

Generating lasting improvements measured well beyond any single project's end date.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Our Values

Rooted in Knowledge

Evidence over assumption; research, evidence, and continuous learning. Shared knowledge is stronger knowledge.

Guided by Integrity

Trust is earned, one decision at a time. Upholding honesty and ethical stewardship in all decisions.

Inspired by Compassion

Behind every statistic is a human story. Transforming expertise into compassionate public service.

Collaborative

No single institution has all answers. Diverse perspectives create superior, durable solutions.

Driven by Innovation

Discovering better ways of solving meaningful problems beyond just adopting new technologies.

Focused on Impact

Activities are not the goal; measurable, positive changes in people's lives remain our ultimate measure.

Guided by the principles of rahmah (compassion), amanah (trust), ihsan (excellence), and maslahah (public benefit).
