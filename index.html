PURPOSE & MISSION

Why We Exist

The hardest problems today aren't solved by one institution alone. Governments need research. Communities need innovation. Technology needs ethics. Public trust needs evidence.

"RKD Foundation exists to connect these strengths — building spaces where knowledge moves from research to classroom to community, and back again."

Yayasan Ruang Karya Berdampak (RKD Foundation) is an Indonesian nonprofit organization dedicated to transforming knowledge into meaningful impact through education, research, innovation, humanitarian action, and cross-sector collaboration.

OUR ECOSYSTEM

Three Initiatives, One Purpose

Rather than operating as a single isolated program, RKD serves as a collaborative platform connecting multiple initiatives with complementary missions.

Klinik Digital

Digital & AI Literacy

Helping Indonesians think clearly in a noisy digital world. Advanced digital literacy, AI ethics, public communication, and fighting misinformation since 2018.

Vokasi Bisa

Vocational & Future-Ready Skills

Preparing young talent for jobs that don't exist yet. Equipping vocational students with future-oriented technical skills, digital capability, and leadership.
