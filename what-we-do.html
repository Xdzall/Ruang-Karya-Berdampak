WHAT WE DO

One Foundation, Three Pathways

RKD Foundation doesn't run one program — it builds an ecosystem. Each initiative stands on its own, with its own name, focus, and community. Together, they share one goal: turning knowledge into public benefit.

INITIATIVE I

Klinik Digital

Clarity in a Complex Digital World

Founded in 2018, when Indonesia's digital transformation was outpacing its digital literacy, Klinik Digital was built to close that gap — starting with a simple mission: help people tell information from misinformation, and use technology without being used by it.

Today it works with ministries, universities, schools, and technology companies to teach digital literacy, AI literacy, and public communication through books, workshops, campaigns, and policy dialogue that have reached communities nationwide.

Digital & AI Literacy Media Literacy Public & Crisis Communication Cyber Awareness Policy Research

Vision: An Indonesia that navigates the digital era with confidence, not confusion.

INITIATIVE II

Vokasi Bisa

Skills for Jobs That Don't Exist Yet

Industries are changing faster than curricula. Vokasi Bisa exists so vocational students and young professionals graduate ready for what's next — not just what's now — combining technical skill with digital fluency, entrepreneurship, and leadership.

Career Readiness Future & Digital Skills Entrepreneurship Industry Partnerships Professional Certification

Vision: Graduates who create opportunities, not just seek them.

Partner with Vokasi Bisa
INITIATIVE III

Jejak Baik

Every Trace of Good Counts

Real change starts small — one volunteer, one tree planted, one act of kindness that spreads. Jejak Baik turns that instinct into a movement, giving individuals, schools, and businesses real ways to give back — from disaster relief to environmental action to community education.

Volunteering Environmental Action Disaster Response Community Education Philanthropy

Philosophy: Kindness shouldn't be extraordinary. It should be culture.
